# English Academic Writing Rules

## Purpose
Guidelines for AI Agents assisting in English academic paper writing, ensuring rigorous style, logical cohesion, active voice, and topic-sentence-first paragraph structure.

## 1. Paragraph Structure: Topic Sentence First
- **Topic Sentence**: Every paragraph MUST begin with a clear topic sentence that states the main claim or thesis of the paragraph.
- **Supporting Sentences**: Follow the topic sentence immediately with evidence, data, citations, or examples.
- **Concluding Sentence**: End the paragraph with a sentence connecting to the overall thesis or transitioning to the next paragraph.

## 2. Voice and Agency
- **Active Voice**: Prefer active voice with clear subjects (e.g. `"We argue that..."`, `"Smith demonstrates..."`, `"Our experiments show..."`). Avoid overly passive constructions like `"It is argued by us..."`.
- **Modality and Precision**: Use precise citation verbs reflecting the exact degree of certainty:
  - Assertions: `argues`, `claims`, `asserts`
  - Evidenced proofs: `demonstrates`, `proves`, `establishes`
  - Suggestions: `suggests`, `indicates`, `implies`

## 3. Cohesion and Signposting
- Use explicit logical signposts at the beginning of sentences (`"However,"`, `"Furthermore,"`, `"Consequently,"`, `"In contrast,"`).
- Ensure every claim is immediately paired with a citation or empirical data.

## 4. Title Page, Author Formatting, and Deliverable Naming Rules
- **No Author Label Rule**: On title pages or headers, do NOT prefix author names with labels like `"Author:"` or `"著者："`. Write author name(s) directly under the title/subtitle (e.g. `Jane Q. Author`).
- **Clean Date Rule**: Avoid verbose labels like `"Submission Date:"`. Format submission dates cleanly as `Month YYYY` or `YYYY年M月`.
- **Official Paper Title Filename Rule**: Default output filenames for compiled deliverables (Markdown / HTML / PDF) MUST match the exact official paper title (e.g. `Official Paper Title.pdf`).
