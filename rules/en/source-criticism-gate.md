# Source Criticism and Context Safeguard Rule

## Purpose
Guidelines for AI Agents analyzing, summarizing, or citing primary and secondary literature to prevent out-of-context quoting (quote mining) and overinterpretation.

## Audit Checklist
1. **Context Safeguard**: Verify that cited quotes reflect the primary author's overall thesis, not an isolated exception.
2. **Philological Validity**: Guard against anachronistic interpretations of historical or technical terms.
3. **Evidence Strength**: Ensure single case studies are not overgeneralized to universal claims.
